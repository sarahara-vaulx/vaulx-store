REQUIREMENTS
What you'll need to be approved
📄

Valid resale certificate or seller's permit REQUIRED

Issued by the state where your business is registered. This lets us sell to you tax-exempt for resale. Upload a copy or provide your certificate number in the form — we keep it on file as required for sales tax exemption records.

🪪

Business registration or EIN REQUIRED

An EIN, state business license, or equivalent registration in your country. Sole proprietors may use an SSN-linked business registration.

🛒

Active marketplace seller account REQUIRED

A live storefront on Walmart, eBay, Amazon, TikTok Shop, or your own site. Share the URL so we can verify your selling history.

📦

Minimum first order: $150

Mix and match across any products. Reorders have no minimum once you're approved.

🚫

Brand and listing conditions

Products must be listed as new and unaltered. Do not repackage, relabel, or claim authorized-distributor status for any brand we carry. Some brands restrict marketplace resale — verify each platform's rules before listing.
